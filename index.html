About

A little about me

I'm Noor — a front-end web development student learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a particular soft spot for UI design. I care about the small details: the way a hover state feels, the rhythm of type on a page, the moment an animation lands just right.

Outside of code, I write — poetry, short fiction, the occasional essay. I like projects that let both sides of me show up at once: logical structure paired with a strong aesthetic point of view.
