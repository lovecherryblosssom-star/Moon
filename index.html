About Me

Where Code
Meets Craft

I'm Noor — a front-end web development student drawn to the space where interface design meets storytelling. I care about typography, spacing, and the small interactions that make a site feel considered rather than assembled, and I'm increasingly pulled toward branding and how a visual language can carry a voice.

Writing is where that voice starts. Poetry, essays, short fiction, copywriting, the occasional attempt at a novel — words are where I work out ideas before they ever become an interface. I like projects that let both sides show up: clean, logical code paired with real creative problem-solving.
