About Me

Where Code
Meets Craft

I'm Noor, a front-end web development student who builds full, multi-page websites from scratch — structuring layouts, writing the interactions, and getting the small details right: responsive breakpoints, clean markup, animations that respond to scroll and cursor instead of just sitting there. HTML, CSS, and JavaScript are where I spend most of my time, and I'm increasingly drawn to UI/UX and branding — how a well-structured interface and a clear visual language work together.

Alongside development, I write — poetry, essays, short fiction, copywriting, the occasional attempt at a novel. It's a different kind of problem-solving, but it feeds the same instinct: get the structure right, then make it feel considered. I like projects that let both sides show up — clean, logical code paired with a genuine creative point of view.
